## 1. The three exams at a glance

### CAT
- 3 sections, **40 min each** (locked order): VARC → DILR → QA
- ~24 / ~22 / ~22 questions · +3 correct, **−1 wrong** (MCQ), no negative on TITA
- Fixed date (~late Nov), single attempt/year

### GMAT (Focus Edition)
- 3 sections, **45 min each**, 64 questions, ~2h 15m · score **205–805**
- **Quantitative Reasoning** (21 q): problem solving — arithmetic & algebra heavy, **geometry minimal**, no data sufficiency here
- **Verbal Reasoning** (23 q): Reading Comprehension + **Critical Reasoning** — *no sentence correction*
- **Data Insights** (20 q): data sufficiency, multi-source reasoning, table analysis, graphics interpretation, two-part analysis
- Adaptive · can bookmark & edit up to 3 answers/section · **book anytime, retake-friendly** (up to 5x/year)

### IAT (IIM-A Aptitude Test) — *verify exact format*
- Assumed: quant + verbal + logical reasoning + data interpretation
- Largely covered by the shared core; add LR depth (see GMAT Critical Reasoning, which transfers well)

## 4. Phase 0 — Diagnostic (Jun 17 – Jun 21)

- Take **one full CAT mock** and **one official GMAT Focus mock** (free on mba.com).
- Tag each area **Strong / Average / Weak**. (As an IITian, expect quant strong — invest the saved time in Verbal/CR and Data Insights.)
- Set up two permanent tools: an **error log** (spreadsheet: question type → why missed → fix) and a growing **concept/formula sheet**.

---

## 5. Phase 1 — Foundations (Jun 22 – Aug 16)

Cover every concept once. No full mocks. Build the shared base that serves all three exams.

**Quant (serves CAT QA / GMAT Quant / IAT):**
1. Number systems → 2. **Arithmetic** (highest weight everywhere — percentages, ratio, P&L, averages, mixtures, TSD, time-work, SI/CI) → 3. Algebra (equations, inequalities, functions, logs, progressions) → 4. Geometry & mensuration *(heavier for CAT, light for GMAT)* → 5. Modern math (P&C, probability, sets)

**Verbal (serves all):**
- **Daily reading** (non-negotiable): 2–3 dense long-form articles — *Aeon, The Economist, The Guardian Long Read, The Hindu editorials, Nautilus*. Write a 2-line summary + the author's main argument each time. Builds RC across all exams.
- CAT-only: para jumbles, para summary, odd-one-out.
- **GMAT Critical Reasoning** — start now: assumptions, strengthen/weaken, inference, flaw, boldface. (PowerScore CR Bible.) This also boosts IAT logical reasoning.

**Data/Logic (serves CAT DILR / GMAT Data Insights / IAT DI):**
- Set types: tables, charts, caselets, Venn, arrangements, games.
- **GMAT Data Insights add-ons:** data sufficiency logic, multi-source reasoning, two-part analysis, graphics interpretation.
- Core skill: **set selection** (CAT) and **DS reasoning** (GMAT) — train both.

---

## 6. Phase 2 — Application (Aug 17 – Oct 11)

Convert concepts to speed + accuracy. **Book and take your GMAT (~early Oct).**

- **Quant:** timed sets, <2 min/question. Drill GMAT-style word problems *and* CAT-style geometry/number theory separately.
- **Verbal:** 3–4 RC passages daily under time; CR sets daily for GMAT/IAT.
- **Data Insights / DILR:** 2 timed sets daily; obsess over *selection* and DS accuracy.
- **Mocks:** 1 GMAT Focus mock/week in Sep, building to your real GMAT attempt. Analyze each at 2× the test time.
- **Milestone:** sit the GMAT once you're consistently hitting your target band in mocks. Retake later if needed — it's allowed and expected.

---

## 7. Phase 3 — Mock intensive (Oct 12 – Nov 15)

- **CAT:** 2–3 full mocks/week, same time-of-day as your slot. Lock per-section game plans (DILR set-ranking in first 3 min; QA two-pass; VARC RC-first).
- **GMAT retake** here if your first score fell short.
- **IAT:** prep + attempt per the BPGP cycle.
- Accuracy > volume: 90% accuracy at 20 attempts usually beats 70% at 28.

---

## 8. Phase 4 — Final taper (Nov 16 – Nov 29)

No new topics. Revise error logs + formula sheets, rehearse timing rules, fix logistics (admit card, ID, reporting time), sleep. Day before: rest.

## 8.5 Phase 5 & 6 — Application, IAT & Interview Prep (Nov 30, 2026 – Jun 2027)

After CAT, the focus shifts to the formal application, the IIMA Admission Test (IAT) (if not submitting GMAT/CAT), and interviews.

### BPGP 2027 Admission Cycle Milestones (Tentative)
- **Dec 15, 2026:** Application portal opens. Start compiling documents.
- **Jan – Mar 2027:** Write Statement of Purpose (SOP) and request Letters of Recommendation (LoR).
- **Early Apr 2027:** Application Submission Deadline.
- **Early May 2027:** IIMA Admission Test (IAT) held for candidates without GMAT/CAT or those wanting another attempt.
- **Mid May 2027:** Shortlist for interviews announced.
- **June 2027:** Personal Interviews (PI).
- **Late June / Early July 2027:** Final admission offers.
- **Late July 2027:** Cohort classes commence.

### Application Strategy
1. **Resume Refinement:** Frame your 3+ years of work experience to highlight leadership, quantitative capability, and business impact. Keep it clean and single-paged.
2. **Statement of Purpose (SOP):** Craft a compelling story explaining:
   - Why MBA and why specifically BPGP at IIM Ahmedabad?
   - How your background aligns with the program.
   - Your career goals post-graduation.
3. **Letters of Recommendation (LoR):** Secure recommendations from professional managers/clients who can attest to your technical skills, leadership potential, and work ethic.

### IAT & Interview Prep
- **IAT Prep:** If taking the IAT in May, revise the Quant, Verbal, and LR foundations from Phase 1. The IAT focuses on logical aptitude and management-oriented problem-solving.
- **Interview Prep:** Focus on industry trends, details of your current job projects, basic business awareness, and resume-based behavioral questions. Practice mock interviews.
